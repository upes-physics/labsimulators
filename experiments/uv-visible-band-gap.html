Principle and equations

A UV–Visible spectrometer compares transmitted intensity I with incident intensity I₀. The optical band gap is obtained by extrapolating the linear Tauc region to the energy axis.

A = −log₁₀(I/I₀),   T = 10−A
α = 2.303A/x
hν (eV) = 1240/λ (nm)
(αhν)² = C(hν − Eg)

Interpretation. The fitted line is y = mhν + c; therefore its energy-axis intercept is Eg = −c/m.
